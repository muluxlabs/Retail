# 14. Questions and answers

## Signing in and access

**I can't see a screen a colleague can see.**
Your role, or your personal access, does not include it. Ask an administrator to look at **Staff › Access** next to your name.

**I was given access but still can't see it.**
Refresh the page. Access applies on the next click; the menu updates when the page reloads.

**My account is locked.**
Eight wrong passwords lock it for 15 minutes. Wait, or ask an administrator to **Reset password**.

## At the till

**"Choose which till is taking this sale."**
The branch has more than one till; pick yours at the top right. It is remembered on this computer.

**"Open your shift before selling."**
Your business requires shifts. **Open shift**, count the cash in the till, and carry on.

**"… is on Tariro's shift."**
Someone else has that till. Use your own till, or ask them (or a supervisor) to close their shift.

**The item scans but says "no price".**
It has not been priced. Someone with price access can set it on the line, or in **Prices**.

**"…: 0 on hand, 2 requested."**
The records say the stock is not there. A manager can authorise the sale; then the item should be counted.

**The customer's account payment is refused.**
It would take them over their credit limit. The message says how much credit is left.

**The internet dropped while I pressed Complete sale.**
Press it again when the connection is back. The same sale is recognised; you get the same receipt, not a second sale.

**How do I reprint a receipt?**
**This session** on the right of the Sell screen → **Reprint**. Older receipts: **Reports › Sales receipts** (with sales access).

## Stock

**Stock on hand is negative.**
Something was sold that the records said was not there (an authorised override), or a delivery has not been recorded yet. Check **Exceptions** and the item's **stock ledger**, then do a **stock take** for it.

**I counted an item wrong in a stock take.**
Posted counts are never edited. Count it again and post the correct figure; the ledger shows both.

**A transfer arrived short.**
Receive what actually arrived. The shortfall is costed and raised as *lost in transit*.

## Money

**A cashier's till was short.**
It is on their shift report and in **Exceptions** (*Till over / short on a shift*), with the amount and their name. Clear it with a note once investigated.

**We paid a supplier twice by mistake.**
Void one payment with a reason (**Buying › Payments** → the payment → **Void**). Nothing is deleted; the void is recorded.

**Can a Z report be reopened?**
No. A mistake found after closing is corrected in the next day's records (for example a count or an adjustment), so both days stay true.

## Prices and suppliers

**A supplier's price list line didn't match.**
The code on their list is not one of your barcodes or SKUs yet. Add the barcode to the item, or ask for your SKU on the list. Once a line is applied with their own code, it matches automatically next time.

**Can a price list lower my prices?**
Yes — with **Keep each item's margin**, a lower cost suggests a lower price. You see and approve every change before it is applied.

## Everything else

**Who changed this price (or this setting, or this person's access)?**
**Exceptions › Audit log**: filter by *kind of change*, or search for the item or value. Each entry shows who, when, and the value before and after. See [the audit log](09-reports-and-controls.md#the-audit-log).

**What is the difference between an exception and an audit entry?**
An exception is something unusual waiting for someone's decision; the audit log is the complete history of changes. See [Exceptions and the audit log](09-reports-and-controls.md#exceptions-and-the-audit-log--what-is-the-difference).

**Who do we call?**
Your system administrator first; they can reset passwords and access. For anything else, your support contact.
